Family friendly Level 1

We love for our families to come out and enjoy the outdoors and partake in the historic adventure that is Wish Washy Rafting. We offer a family friendly course that let's even the littlest friends enjoy a rafting experience.

Family Friendly Level 2

This is for the brave of heart. While still maintaining a safe family experience for those of all ages, this rafting experience will get you wet. You will begin to experience the turbulance and fun that earned Wishy Washy Rafting our name.

White Water Level 1

This is a more experienced adventure. Thos life jackets will start coming in hand for those of you that can't hold on. This adventure is two full hours of fun. Exciting twists and turns will keep you on your toes as you make your way down the rapids.

White Water Level 2

For those of you brave enough to enlist in the level 2 adventure, we'll be needing those signed waivers. You'll get to experience the bubbling waters up close and personal. There's not just one or two sudden drops into turbulent water, there are seven gut-wrenching turns that are bound to knock someone out of the raft. For this experience you aren't paying for the time out on the water, but rather the thrill of constant adrenaline coursing through your veins.
